Silicom Ltd. — Technology / cloud_datacenter_network

Silicom Ltd. designs, manufactures, markets, and supports networking and data infrastructure solutions in the United States, North America, Israel, Europe, and the Asia Pacific. It provides server network interface cards; and smart card products, including encryption, data compression, other hardware acceleration and offloading cards, and field programmable gate-array based cards. The company also provides smart platforms, such as virtualized customer-premises equipment and universal customer-premises equipment; and edge devices for SD-WAN, SASE, telco dedicated routers, and NFV.
