Company snapshot

Keel Infrastructure Corp. — Keel Infrastructure Corp. operates digital and energy infrastructure with focus on high-performance computing (HPC) and artificial intelligence (AI) workloads in North America, Canada, and the United States. It primarily owns and operates data centers housing computers to validate transactions on the bitcoin blockchain, as well as sells computational power used for hashing calculations for cryptocurrency mining. The company also provides electrician services to commercial and residential customers in Quebec, Canada. It also undertakes hosting of third-party mining hardware. The company was founded in 2017 and is based in New York, New York.
