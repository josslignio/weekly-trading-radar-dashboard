Keel Infrastructure Corp. operates digital and energy infrastructure with focus on high-performance computing (HPC) and artificial intelligence (AI) workloads in North America, Canada, and the United States. It primarily owns and operates data centers housing computers to validate transactions on the bitcoin blockchain, as well as sells computational power used for hashing calculations for cryptocurrency mining.
